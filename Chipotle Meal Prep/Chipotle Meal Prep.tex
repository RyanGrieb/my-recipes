\section*{Instructions}

\begin{enumerate}

    \item \textbf{Prep Everything} \\
          Thinly slice half the red onion and finely dice the rest. Grate 12 garlic cloves and finely chop 4 more. Roughly chop the chipotle peppers, dice the white onion, slice the serrano, and chop the tomato and all the cilantro. Zest and juice the lime, and measure out everything marked with a spoon.

    \item \textbf{For the Pickled Red Onion} \\
          Put the sliced onion in a 1-quart heatproof jar. Bring the water, vinegar, and salt to a boil in a saucepan, then pour over the onion to cover. Cool to room temperature uncovered, then seal and refrigerate (keeps for a few months).

    \item \textbf{For the Rice} \\
          Rinse the rice under cold water until it runs clear, strain, and cook in a rice cooker with the 3 cups of water. Stir in the cilantro, butter, lime zest, and juice, season with salt, and keep warm.

    \item \textbf{For the Chicken} \\
          Mix the chopped chipotles and their adobo with the salt and garlic. Pour over the chicken thighs in a bowl and coat evenly. Marinate 30 mins to overnight. \\
          Heat a cast iron/grill pan over medium-high heat with oil. Sear chicken in batches (don't overcrowd) for 2–3 mins per side until charred. Cook to 165$^\circ$F. If charring too fast, finish in oven at 375$^\circ$F. Rest 5 mins before dicing.

    \item \textbf{For the Black Beans} \\
          In a saucepan over medium heat with oil, sauté onion, serrano, and tomato until softened (2–3 mins). Stir in black beans, stock, and salt. Boil then reduce to low; simmer until veggies soften and stock reduces by a quarter. Remove from heat and stir in garlic.

    \item \textbf{For the Corn Relish} \\
          Char corn over open flame/grill (1–2 mins/side). Char jalapeños until black; seed and peel if desired, and dice. Cut corn off cob. Mix corn, jalapeño, red onion, cilantro. Season with salt/pepper. Add vinegar to taste.

    \item \textbf{For the Spicy Mayo} \\
          Whisk all ingredients in a bowl. Season with salt. Refrigerate.

    \item \textbf{To Assemble} \\
          Divide rice between bowls (about 1 cup each). Top with diced chicken, corn relish (with spicy mayo), black beans, pickled red onions, cheese, and cilantro. Enjoy!

\end{enumerate}
