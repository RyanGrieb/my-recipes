\section*{Instructions}

\begin{enumerate}

    \item \textbf{Prep Everything} \\
          Thinly slice half the red onion and finely dice the rest. Grate 12 garlic cloves and finely chop 4 more. Roughly chop the chipotles, dice the white onion, slice the serrano, and chop the tomato and all the cilantro. Zest and juice the lime, rinse the rice, and measure out everything marked with a spoon.

    \item \textbf{For the Pickled Red Onion} \\
          Put the sliced onion in a 1-quart heatproof jar. Boil the water, vinegar, and salt, then pour over the onion. Cool uncovered, then seal and refrigerate (keeps for months).

    \item \textbf{For the Rice} \\
          Bring the rice and water to a boil. Cover and cook on low until the water is absorbed (18--20 minutes). Rest covered off the heat (5 minutes), then stir in the cilantro, butter, lime zest, and juice. Salt to taste and keep warm.

    \item \textbf{For the Chicken} \\
          Mix the chipotles and adobo with the salt and garlic, toss with the chicken, and marinate (30 minutes to overnight). Sear in batches in an oiled cast iron or grill pan over medium-high heat until charred (2--3 minutes per side) and 165$^\circ$F inside, finishing in a 375$^\circ$F oven if it chars too fast. Rest 5 minutes, then dice.

    \item \textbf{For the Black Beans} \\
          Sauté the onion, serrano, and tomato in the oil over medium heat until softened (2--3 minutes). Add the beans, stock, and salt, boil, then simmer on low until the stock reduces by a quarter. Off the heat, stir in the garlic.

    \item \textbf{For the Corn Relish} \\
          Broil the corn and jalapeños on a foil-lined sheet pan on the top rack, turning often, until charred in spots (8--12 minutes). Dice the jalapeños (seed and peel them if you like), cut the kernels off the cobs, and mix with the red onion and cilantro. Season with salt, pepper, and vinegar to taste.

    \item \textbf{For the Spicy Mayo} \\
          Whisk everything together, season with salt, and refrigerate until needed.

    \item \textbf{To Assemble} \\
          Divide the rice between six bowls (about 1 cup each). Top with diced chicken, corn relish (with spicy mayo), black beans, pickled red onions, cheese, and cilantro. Enjoy!

\end{enumerate}
