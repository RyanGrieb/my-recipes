      \item Unsalted Butter – 1 tbsp (14g)

      \item[\measure] Ground Turmeric – 2½ tsp (8g)

\section*{Instructions}

\begin{enumerate}

      \item \textbf{Prep Everything} \\
            Dice the chicken and onion. Finely chop the parsley, dice the tomatoes, slice the green onions, and mince the garlic. Juice both lemons, rinse the rice, and measure out everything marked with a spoon.

      \item \textbf{Marinate the Chicken} \\
            Combine the chicken with the rest of the chicken ingredients. Mix well and let it marinate while you heat the pan.

      \item \textbf{Cook the Chicken} \\
            Heat a large pan or griddle over medium heat with a splash of oil. Cook the chicken in batches (don't overcrowd the pan) for 8--10 minutes, until charred and cooked through to 165$^\circ$F (74$^\circ$C). Set aside to rest. \\
            Wipe the pan clean between batches so leftover marinade doesn't burn.

      \item \textbf{Make the Pilaf Rice} \\
            While the chicken cooks, melt the butter in a pot over low heat. Add the onion and cook until soft (4--5 minutes). Stir in the turmeric, paprika, cumin, and salt, and toast for 1 minute. Add the rinsed rice and stir until every grain is coated in the spiced butter. \\
            Pour in the chicken stock and bring to a boil. Cover, reduce to low, and cook for 12 minutes. Take the pot off the heat and let it rest with the lid on for 4 minutes.

      \item \textbf{Make the Tabbouleh} \\
            While the rice cooks, combine all the tabbouleh ingredients in a bowl. Refrigerate until needed.

      \item \textbf{Make the Garlic Sauce} \\
            Whisk together the yogurt, sriracha, garlic, paprika, onion powder, salt, and pepper until smooth. Taste and add more sriracha if you want extra heat.

      \item \textbf{Portion and Store} \\
            Divide the rice and chicken between 5 meal-prep containers (750ml each). Pack the tabbouleh and garlic sauce in separate small containers so they stay fresh.

\end{enumerate}
